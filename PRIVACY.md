# BrickController Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

BrickController does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

App settings, controller profiles, connected-device information, and creations are stored locally on the user's device. Bluetooth communication is used only to discover and control compatible brick devices.

The optional HTTP control service is disabled by default. When enabled, it runs locally on the user's device and may be made available on the user's local network according to the listen mode selected by the user. Access tokens generated by this feature remain under the user's control and are not sent to the developer.

Apple and the operating system may process information independently under their own privacy policies, for example when downloading the app from the App Store.

Questions about this policy may be sent to raphael.volz@hs-pforzheim.de.
